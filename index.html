About KaleGame Studio

KaleGame Studio is a one-person indie studio born from the dream of a soon-to-be graduate. What started as a student's passion project is now a dedicated journey to craft fun, cozy, and meaningful game worlds.

Wearing every hat—from programming to pixel art—I pour my heart into every detail. My goal is simple: to create interesting, nostalgic experiences that bring a smile to your face and offer a relaxing escape from the everyday.

Beast Ville

A perfect place to chill after working hours and explore an open, untamed world. Here, you will hand-revive creatures that once roamed the Earth—from a tiny chicken to a colossal dinosaur. Beyond that, everything from farming and resource gathering to intense combat is fully brought to life within the game. Follow our project now to receive the earliest updates and announcements!

Beast Ville: The First Stand

Long before the world of Beast Ville, a defensive war erupted as creatures of the Earth fought to hold the line against invading entities from the void. Your mission is to survive for as many rounds as possible, wielding a deck of randomized cards dealt to your hand. The real excitement lies in the secret interactions that will be unveiled as you play. Continuous updates are on the way!
